The lineup

Every app, one purpose each.

Three apps, three skills. All free to download on the App Store, all built around short daily practice.

Music · Practice

Daily Metronome

A complete practice companion for musicians learning piano, guitar, violin, drums, and more. Keep time, import your sheet music, record your sessions, and build a practice habit that sticks.

  • Precise, reliable metronome
  • Import PDF sheet music
  • Practice recordings & playback
  • Saved tempo presets
  • Practice trainers & progress dashboards (Pro)
  • Automatic page turning (Pro)

NoteSwan: Learn Sight Reading

Level up your music skills with a clean, simple, and intuitive way to learn to read music. Whether you're starting piano or brushing up on theory, NoteSwan turns sight reading into a daily game.

  • Learn to sight-read notes fast
  • Master scales & chords
  • Chord progression training
  • Rhythm & interval practice
  • On-screen piano keyboard
  • Progress tracking & stats

Language · Education

Kana Flashcards

Built for one purpose: helping you master Japanese kana quickly and efficiently. Learn hiragana and katakana with flashcards and spaced repetition, then lock it in with typing practice.

  • Hiragana & katakana flashcards
  • Spaced repetition engine
  • Kana typing practice
  • Weak-kana targeted review
  • Daily streaks & rewards
  • Progress stats & achievements
