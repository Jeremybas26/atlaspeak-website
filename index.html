Meet the maker

Hi, I'm Jeremy.

Piano learner. Kana driller.
One-person app studio.

Atlas Peak is just me — a self-taught developer in New York City. I spend my evenings building small tools for the daily things I care about: staying focused, playing piano, and learning Japanese. I kept wishing the apps I used were simpler and less bloated, so I built my own.

Every app here started as a tool I actually needed. Breadcrumbs is for keeping habits moving one checkpoint at a time. Daily Metronome lives on my piano's music stand. Kana Flashcards is how I'm learning Japanese, one train ride at a time. I design, code, and answer every support email myself — there's no team, no investors, no growth hacks.

I'm not a company trying to maximize your screen time. I'm a fellow climber — a few steps ahead on some trails, a few steps behind on others. If one of these apps helps your daily practice, that genuinely makes my day.

Questions, ideas, or just want to say hi? atlaspeak@atlaspeakapp.com
