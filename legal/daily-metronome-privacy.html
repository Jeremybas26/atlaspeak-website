Daily Metronome — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atlas Peak ("we", "us") handles information when you use Daily Metronome ("the App"). We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run and improve the App.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Your learning and practice data — such as progress, statistics, streaks, settings, imported sheet music, and practice recordings — is stored locally on your device. Deleting the App deletes this data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Usage & diagnostics: We use Google Firebase to collect anonymized analytics (such as feature usage and session length) and crash reports so we can fix bugs and improve the App.
  • Purchase information: Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

3. How We Use Data

We use collected data only to operate the App, diagnose crashes, understand which features are used, and improve future updates. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (analytics & crash reporting)
  • Apple (App Store, payments)

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Analytics data is retained by Firebase per Google's standard retention periods. Your practice data lives on your device and is deleted when you delete the App. To request deletion of any data associated with you, email atlaspeak@atlaspeakapp.com.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions? Email atlaspeak@atlaspeakapp.com.
