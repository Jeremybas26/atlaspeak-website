Privacy Choices & Data Requests

GeoPassport · Updated October 14, 2026

Control device permissions

In iPhone Settings, review GeoPassport's Location, Photos, Camera, Contacts, and Notifications permissions. Location can be approximate or precise; background access is a separate permission. Manage Health access in the Health app or device privacy settings. Revoking a permission stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously saved records.

Control sharing

Use the relevant GeoPassport memory, passport, story, trip, and friend-location settings to review audiences and stop sharing. Use block controls when appropriate. People who already received content may have saved copies.

Delete an account or request data removal

Profile settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account removes your sign-in account. For associated cloud records and uploaded content, email atlaspeak@atlaspeakapp.com with the subject “GeoPassport Data Deletion.” Include your username and a way to contact you; avoid sending private photos, authentication codes, or passwords. We will verify ownership and explain any information that cannot be removed immediately. Local app files and copies held by recipients are separate from cloud deletion. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Access, export, correction, and other privacy requests

Email atlaspeak@atlaspeakapp.com with the subject “GeoPassport Privacy Request” and describe what you would like to access, export, correct, or restrict. If you cannot sign in, contact us for help; do not create a second account solely to request deletion.

Read the Privacy Policy for data categories, providers, and retention, or visit Support.
