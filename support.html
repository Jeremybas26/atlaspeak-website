Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel or manage a subscription?

Subscriptions (like Breadcrumbs Pro or Daily Metronome Pro) are billed through your Apple ID, not by Atlas Peak directly. To manage or cancel: open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → select the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase, then look for a Restore Purchases button in the app's settings screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so nothing is lost when you switch devices.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds for App Store purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you'd like refunded.

How does Breadcrumbs iCloud backup work?

Breadcrumbs can use iCloud to keep streaks and habit progress safer across devices. Make sure iCloud is enabled for the app in iOS Settings and that you are signed in with the same Apple ID on each device. If progress looks out of date, keep the app open for a moment on Wi-Fi and try again.

What should I include in a Breadcrumbs bug report?

Please include your device model, iOS version, whether you were using widgets or Live Activities, the habit/session name if relevant, and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots are welcome if they make the problem easier to understand.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely — feature requests are the main way our apps improve. Email atlaspeak@atlaspeakapp.com with the app name and your idea. Since Atlas Peak is an independent studio, good ideas often ship quickly.

How is my data handled?

Our apps store your practice data on your device and, where applicable, use standard services like iCloud, Firebase, or Google AdMob. See each app's privacy policy on the Terms & Privacy page for full details, including how to request data deletion.

Will Atlas Peak apps come to Android?

Right now we're focused on making the best possible iOS experience. If Android versions become available, they'll be announced here on atlaspeakapp.com.
